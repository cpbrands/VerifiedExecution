## Context

VE-001 delegates schema and concrete representation responsibilities while preserving Action meaning and occurrence/content/authority boundaries. The three successor Drafts make exact source, descriptor, normalization and scoped external responsibilities explicit. The assessed draft-1 proposals are publicly accessible, not Approved contracts or final adopted agreeing publication/context bindings.

The assessed design target is the three draft-1 source files at [immutable commit 313a2f3](https://github.com/cpbrands/VerifiedExecution/tree/313a2f3eba707aa901dbeba51a327bc32081fcce). The exact descriptor-local version 2 and sixteen interpretation-source selections remain the baseline. UGD-1 is the coordinator's local label for the owner-adopted bounded UTF-8/NFC finding stated in decision item 2, not another allocated publication or byte-function input. The Founder accepted this ADR and RFC-014's scoped additive design/Class B classification; that acceptance alone does not approve the specifications or authorize implementation/release operations.

## Decision — accepted within the recorded scope

The Founder, acting in the declared authorized successor-owner capacity, accepted the following design decision:

1. Introduce the canonical successor, bounded Lynx schema successor and common annex as additional, independently selected subordinate contracts, with the RFC's **Class B** classification at that additive boundary.
2. Retain the already-adopted UGD-1 source-text finding for this exact bounded pair: the numeric UTF-8 definitions agree and its selected Unicode 6.2 NFC application does not require changing the fixed core. Do not infer general Unicode-edition equivalence or old/new byte equality.
3. Retain the approved complete S/native mapping, per-input FIPS SHA-256 definition/domain, fixed schema field semantics, scoped Lynx/ISO restatement, exact selected source roles and fixed-import conflict veto. No technical alteration is made by this decision record.
4. Preserve all historical publications, contexts, Type/selector meanings, expectations and reviews. Do not select a successor through an old identifier, matching kind, shape or raw octets. Define no automatic translation or migration.
5. Keep fixed core sources unchanged. Require independently agreeing successor publication/context selections through the existing annex → canonical → schema → external-context arrangement, without self-pin or future-peer cycles.
6. Keep Draft publication, governance design acceptance, exactly bound review/support targets, specification maturity and interoperability as separate states. Acceptance of this ADR does not itself promote a specification or discharge RFC-004's Approved-profile prerequisite.

The design choice is additive, not a finding that every successor rule has identical historical meaning. A replacement, forced migration, changed existing admission/equality or fixed-core conflict requires a separate classification/change proposal, including Class C where applicable.

## Alternatives

- Retain only the old sources: preserves history but does not supply the new explicit contract.
- Rewrite or reinterpret old contracts as editorial: rejected because it hides identity/selection changes and assumes unproved equivalence.
- Globally revise encoding/core rules or import all external facilities: unnecessary for the scoped design and increases authority/dependency impact.
- Replace old deployments through a breaking migration: not proposed; requires its own justification and migration contract.

## Consequences

The selected successor must stand on its own exact definitions and complete logical descriptor; old outputs cannot fill missing commitments. Consumers must distinguish contract/context identity rather than infer interchangeability. SHA domain bounds remain structural arguments, not conformance results. Full message validity, external authority and settlement truth remain separate claims.

Explicit roles reduce hidden inputs but retain real audit/carriage costs. No fourth normative definition source, generic schema layer or new primitive is introduced merely by these informative governance records.

After acceptance, source integration needs separately scoped authority, actual RFC/ADR references, appropriate revision/history/changelog treatment and rechecking affected bindings. This acceptance does not itself perform or authorize those operations; any later preparation/integration remains separately scoped. Unresolved recursive roles, authentic establishment where claimed and implementation evidence are not closed by decision wording.
